Zones

A zone is an asset container with the zone scene and its content.

Asset Container

Scene_Unity3D with the Main_Scene group is the zone scene. As the zone scene, Springtime003, HomeLotSmall and Dress_your_pet are loaded from the client build, not downloaded.
EmptyScene.unity3d without a group is required in every zone: it has the NPC dialog camera, the radial menus and the minimap.
Text is the zone name, the locked flag and the areas (SpringBay_Zone.txt). The spawn points do not load without it.
Treenode with the SpawnPoints group lists the item spawn point sets (SpringBay_SpawnPoints.txt).
Prefab_Unity3D are the spawn point sets the quests use (CSPS_CenterSquare.unity3d).

Home Yard

The yard has no container, the server sends its scene and EmptyScene itself.
The NPC zone HardCodedZoneId (4, 16, 0, 2937912) is required for the yard NPCs. NPCs are placed in zones on the NPCs page.
