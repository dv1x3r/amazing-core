Site Frame

The site frame is the root asset container with the core assets, UI skins and localization. The client requests it on start and after login, the only known type_value is 1.

Assets are found by res_name, exact and case-sensitive. Files from the cache could have invalid names and must be renamed to the name the client asks for.
The asset type matters only for Audio, Config_Text, LocalizedText, Movie_Unity3D, Nix_Animation_Unity3D, Preload_PrefabUnity3D and Scene_Unity3D.

Core

Player_Base.unity3d is the base Zing rig with shared animations.
PlayerCamera.unity3d is the player camera prefab.
ShadersList.unity3d contains some special shaders. Other shaders are built into the client.

Dressing Room

Dress_your_pet.unity3d is the preloaded dressing room scene.
DressYourZing_Layout.txt is the dressing room UI layout.
Avatar_SlotIds.txt is a slot registry for wearable/equippable avatar parts. Also imported to the avatar_slot SQL table.

Localization

Main_Localization.txt is a localization file for buttons and main text.

World

Sky.unity3d is the cloud layer spawned above zones.
TeleportSmoke.unity3d is the teleport smoke effect.
minimap_base.unity3d is the zone map base mesh and texture.
syst019_arrow.unity3d, syst019_map.unity3d and syst019_shovel.unity3d are the treasure hunt arrow, map and shovel models.
Crown.unity3d is the crown shown above the mob quest leader.

Sounds

dock_up.unity3d and dock_down.unity3d are panel slide sounds.
fanfare.unity3d is the prize wheel win sound.

Quest Rewards

RewardUIPane.unity3d is the quest reward dialog, and RewardItem.unity3d is a reward item row inside it.

NPC and Crafting UI

NPC_Hint.unity3d is the NPC hint bubble.
NPC_Choice.unity3d is an NPC skill entry.
HintBubble.unity3d is the material hint bubble.
MaterialCard.unity3d, RecipeCard.unity3d, TypeCard.unity3d and Skill List.unity3d are crafting journal cards and lists.
CraftingUIPane.unity3d is the crafting dialog.

Fishing and Home Upgrade

Bobber.unity3d is the fishing bobber, and PreloadedFishingSounds.unity3d contains the fishing sounds.
BrickCamera.unity3d is the house upgrade camera animation, and PreloadedHomeUpgrade.unity3d contains the house upgrade sounds.

Nix

NixConfig.txt lists the zone and screen Nix, the spawn timing and the mini-game chances.
NPCtheNix001.unity3d to NPCtheNix006.unity3d are the zone Nix, NixSpawnpoints.unity3d are their spawn points.
ninjaSpawnIn_comboAnim.unity3d and ninjaSpawnOut_comboAnim.unity3d are the spawn smoke, <spawn point>_Loop_comboAnim.unity3d are the spawn point props. They need the Nix_Animation_Unity3D type and exact names.
The hash-named Nix_Animation_Unity3D bundles are the Nix body animations (theNix_* inside), only their type matters.
ScreenNix001.unity3d to ScreenNix006.unity3d are the Nix over mini-games, with ScreenNixSpawnpoints.unity3d and ScreenNixLight.unity3d. ScreenNix004.unity3d is missing from the cache.
nixsounds.unity3d are the Nix sounds, nixShieldExplode.unity3d is the shield explosion.

UI Skins

A skin is a <skin>.txt file and a <texture>.unity3d bundle, the texture name is in the skin file. Without a skin the window has no background.
Task_dialogue_Texture is the NPC speech bubble.
TaskV2_Texture (texture TaskV2_Texture_Image) is the job dialog and the job frame.
Missions_Texture is used by the job dialog.
Task_Complete_Incomplete_Headers_Texture is the job complete and incomplete headers.
Supersnack_Texture.unity3d is the texture for the Supersnack skin built into the client.
