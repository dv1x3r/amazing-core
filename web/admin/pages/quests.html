Quests

A quest is offered by an NPC: a story quest or a job.
Story quests are imported from the cache, a quest with a gap in the cache is skipped.

Asset Container

Treenode is the quest definition: texts, audio, task type, items and finish rules.
The prop from Attach_Asset is held by the player during the task, its res_name must match.

Items

Every item in the quest definition has gsfoid and Item_Asset.
gsfoid is the OID of the Quest Item container, Item_Asset is the res_name of the item bundle in it.
The item icon <Item_Asset>.icon.png and name <Item_Asset>_info go to the same container, they are not found in the cache.
The items spawn at the zone spawn points, see Zones.

Fields

Order: an NPC offers one quest at a time in the grid order.
Repeatable: off for story quests. The NPC offers them first, each until it is completed, then the jobs, and starts the jobs over after the last one.
XP, Coins and NPC Points are the rewards for completing the quest, not given yet.
Cooldown: seconds the NPC offers no quest after this one is completed.

Steps

In a quest with steps the NPC dialog comes from the quest, then the client plays the steps one by one in the grid order, without a dialog.
A step is a quest definition without texts, the cache does not link it to the parent. The import links the steps right after the parent by the OID, with an item of the parent.
The quest is completed when its last step is completed.

Rules

The Quest Item containers and amounts the player collects, the server completes the quest when every amount is collected. A quest without rules and steps is never completed.
The rules of a step are shown when the step is selected, a quest with steps has no rules.
They repeat FinishRules of the quest definition: the client shows its own amounts, keep them the same.

In Game

The quest windows need EmptyScene in the zone and the UI skins in the site frame.
A click on the NPC during its task shows "task in progress".
Cancel on the job frame abandons the task, the NPC offers it again.
