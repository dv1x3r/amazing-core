Quests

A quest is a job offered by an NPC. Only NPC jobs are supported for now.

Asset Container

Treenode is the quest definition: texts, audio, task type, items and finish rules.
The prop from Attach_Asset is held by the player during the task, its res_name must match.

Items

Every item in the quest definition has gsfoid and Item_Asset.
gsfoid is the OID of the Quest Item container, Item_Asset is the res_name of the item bundle in it.
The item icon <Item_Asset>.icon.png and name <Item_Asset>_info go to the same container, they are not found in the cache.
The items spawn at the zone spawn points, see Zones.

Fields

Order: an NPC offers one quest at a time, in the grid order, and starts over after the last one.
XP, Coins and NPC Points are the rewards for completing the quest, not given yet.
Cooldown: seconds the NPC offers no quest after this one is completed.

In Game

The quest windows need EmptyScene in the zone and the UI skins in the site frame.
A click on the NPC during its task shows "task in progress".
Cancel on the job frame abandons the task, the NPC offers it again.
