Items

The item-container mapping used for InventoryManager.

Items may have multiple categories and acceptable avatar slots assigned.

An item with a store theme is sold in the World Shop in that theme, every NPC store sells all items.

A house has the Yard, House and HouseCategory categories and an Interior Container: a bought house gets its interior maze with this container as the home theme. The Scene_Unity3D of the interior is the Interior_N of the house Text treenode, its group is Main_Scene, with EmptyScene next to it. A house given to a player on the Players page gets its interior maze too.

The yard and the house interiors of a player are on the Mazes tab of the Players page. The placement of the player items (Placed In, Position, Attached To, Mount) is changed by the game only.

An item with the Upgrade category is the Upgrade Of a house to the Upgrade Stage (Stage_N of the house treenode). The house sells its next upgrades in GetStoreItems, the stage of a house is the number of upgrades attached to it. The house container has the models and the exterior icons of every stage, the interior container has the interiors of the next stages in the Locked group.
